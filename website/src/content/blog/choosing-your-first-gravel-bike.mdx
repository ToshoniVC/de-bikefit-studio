Gravel is the fastest-growing corner of cycling, and for good reason: one bike that
commutes on Monday and bikepacks on Saturday is hard to argue with. Here's what we
tell every first-time buyer.

## Tyre clearance is king

If you remember one thing, remember this: **clearance buys you versatility**. A frame
that swallows 50mm tyres can run fast 38s for road-heavy days and balloon up for
rough stuff. You can always go narrower — you can't go wider than the frame allows.

## Gearing: go easier than you think

Loaded climbing on loose surfaces is humbling. A 1× setup with a wide-range cassette
keeps things simple and gives you a bailout gear for the steep stuff.

## Geometry sets the mood

- **Racy** geometry is twitchy and fast — great if you came from road.
- **Adventure** geometry is stable and forgiving — better for long, loaded days.
