Buy a bike online from a big box retailer and it arrives in a flat box, half-assembled,
torqued by nobody in particular. Here's what a proper build adds.

## Every bolt to spec

Carbon components have torque limits for a reason. We torque every interface to the
manufacturer's spec and mark it — so you know it's right, and you can see if anything
moves.

## Wheels true, brakes dialled

Out-of-the-box wheels are rarely tensioned evenly. We true and tension every wheel,
bed in the brakes, and make sure the drivetrain shifts cleanly under load.

## A fit that fits you

A bike that's the right size still isn't set up for *you*. Saddle height, reach and
cleat position all get dialled before you roll out the door.

That's the Qarakter difference — and it's why our bikes feel ready from the first pedal stroke.
