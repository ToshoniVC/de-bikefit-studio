Booking

The booking page is where the visitor turns into a customer. It needs to feel as low-friction as the brand promises: simple, calm, step by step.

This is a placeholder. The flow, copy, and integration (calendar, payment, confirmation email) are still to be decided.

What will live here

  1. Service selection. Adult fit, youth fit, family pack, at-home fit. One-line description of each, with duration and starting price.
  2. Date & time. Pulls available slots from the studio calendar.
  3. Your details. Name, email, phone, age (for youth fits, parent's contact too).
  4. Your bike. Type, brand & model if known, any recent changes. Optional — helps us prepare.
  5. What's bothering you. Free-text field for current complaints or goals. Three lines is fine.
  6. Confirm & pay. Deposit or full payment, whichever we decide. Cancellation policy in plain language.
  7. Confirmation email. Date, time, address, what to bring, how to find us. Reply-to is a real person.

Tone notes

  • Use "you" and "we". Never "the client" or "the studio".
  • Short labels. No jargon — "What's bothering you" is better than "Clinical history / symptoms".
  • Reassurance over selling: a small line under the form noting that we'll get back within 24 hours and that the deposit is refundable up to 48h before the appointment.
  • If a youth fit is selected, the form should adapt: contact fields move to the parent, with the child's name and age as separate inputs.

Open questions

  • Calendar tool: built-in, Cal.com, Calendly, or our own?
  • Payment: deposit only, or full upfront? Which provider — Mollie, Stripe?
  • Pricing display: per service on this page, or just on a separate "Prices" page linked here?
  • Insurance / reimbursement note: does any Belgian mutualité contribute? Worth mentioning if yes.
