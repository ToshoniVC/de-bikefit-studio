1. The story · Richard

"My first competition wasn't about results. It was about what a bike can do for you."

Hi, I'm Richard. I've been on a bike for as long as I can remember. Nineteen years of competition, and a lifetime more just riding — to school, to work, into the hills on the weekend, across borders on summer trips.

My first competition wasn't about results or splits or wattages. It was about what a bike could do for me. The places it took me, the people it introduced me to, the way it changed how I moved through the world. That experience is the reason I'm still here, thirty years on, doing this.

And that's what I want every rider to have. Not pain. Not a body that gives up before the road does. Not a bike that fights you. Just the ride — the one that made you fall in love with the bike in the first place.

That's why I set up De Bikefit Studio. Whether you're seven or seventy, racing or commuting, weekend warrior or weekday tourer — everyone deserves to enjoy everything a bike has to offer. My job is to make sure your bike lets you.

2. Frequently asked questions

These answers double as SEO content (each one a likely Google query). Keep them short and honest. If an answer needs more space, it gets its own page and we link from here.

Why is a bike fit important?
A bike that doesn't fit your body costs you in three ways: pain, lost power, and shorter rides. A bike fit aligns the bike to you — not the other way around — so every ride feels better and you stay healthier on the bike for longer.
How often should I get a bike fit?
For most adults, every 18 to 24 months — or whenever something changes. A new bike, an injury, a major weight or flexibility change, or a new riding goal are all good reasons to come back. For children, more often: they grow, the fit doesn't.
How does a bike fit work?
We start with a conversation about your body, your goals, and your complaints. Then we observe you on the bike — posture, pedal stroke, saddle position, movement patterns. We adjust step by step, with feedback after each change, until everything lines up. You leave understanding why your bike is set up the way it is.
Can I have a bike fit at home?
Yes — we offer on-location fits for groups, clubs, and families. For most individual fits the studio gives us a more controlled environment, but if you can't come to us, we come to you. Get in touch and we'll talk through it.
What should I bring to a bike fit?
Your bike (cleaned and in working order), the shoes and cycling clothes you actually ride in, and any notes about what's been bothering you. That's it. We'll handle the rest.
How long does a bike fit take?
Allow 90 minutes for a standard adult fit. A youth fit is shorter — about 60 minutes. Complex cases, first-timers, or post-injury fits may run to two hours. We never rush — when it's done, it's done.
Will I need to buy new parts?
Sometimes. Often not. We always work with what you've already got first; if a saddle, stem, or set of cleats genuinely isn't suitable for your body, we'll tell you — and explain why, with no pressure.
Do you fit electric bikes?
Yes. The principles are the same — the rider's body still has to match the bike. We fit road, gravel, mountain, city, touring, and e-bikes.
