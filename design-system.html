De Bikefit Studio — Design System

The foundations, components and patterns that make up the De Bikefit Studio brand. Quiet, calm, deliberate. Black, white, and grey — never shouty.

01 Principles 02 Voice 03 Logo 04 Color 05 Typography 06 Spacing & layout 07 Components 08 Patterns 09 Imagery 10 Tokens
01 / Principles

Calm, considered, on character

Every design decision should make a recreational cyclist feel welcome and unhurried. We dress the brand in restraint, not performance.

  • QuietUse white space. Avoid loud colors and dense layouts. The reader sets the pace.
  • PersonalNo copy-paste. Show that the studio sees the rider, not the segment.
  • Low-thresholdNever intimidate. No jargon, no judgement, no big claims.
  • Step by stepLead the eye in clear stages. One thought per block, then a pause.
  • SubstantiveBold display type for what matters. Plain body type for everything else.
  • HonestPhotography over render, statement over claim, monochrome over fashion.
02 / Voice & tone

Talk like a person, not a brochure

Dutch first, written for a reader who isn't a cyclist yet. Short sentences. Concrete words. Warm, but never cute.

Do

  • "Fiets zonder pijn."
  • "Simpel, rustig en stap voor stap."
  • "Iedereen heeft een andere aanpak nodig."
  • "Kinderen mogen mee."
  • "Op jouw tempo."

Don't

  • "Optimize your biomechanical efficiency."
  • "Unlock peak performance!"
  • "Industry-leading bikefit experience."
  • "Schedule your consultation today!!!"
  • Excessive exclamation marks or capslock.
03 / Logo

A bike, measured

The mark is a side-profile bicycle with a dashed measurement overlay — a quiet visual nod to what a bikefit actually is. Wordmark in Montserrat 800; subtitle widely tracked.

Primary — on white

Default usage. Use wherever possible.

On surface

For utility bars, page-head, secondary surfaces.

On ink (inverted)

For dark callouts & footer. Production-ready inverted asset to follow.

Clear space & usage

  • Minimum clear space around the mark: equal to the cap height of the wordmark on every side.
  • Minimum digital height: 40px. Below this, set the wordmark on its own line without the icon.
  • Don't recolor. Don't add shadows. Don't outline. Don't rotate. Don't stretch.
  • The dashed "fit-line" overlay is part of the mark — it never appears on its own as a graphic device.
04 / Color

Black, white, and the greys between

The palette is intentionally restrained. No accent color. Hierarchy comes from contrast, scale, and white space — not from hue.

Core

Ink --color-ink #1F1F1F
Ink soft --color-ink-soft #565656
Ink mute --color-ink-mute #888888
Background --color-bg #FFFFFF

Surfaces & lines

Surface --color-surface #F4F4F4
Surface 2 --color-surface-2 #EBEBEB
Line --color-line #E6E6E6
Line strong --color-line-strong #C8C8C8

Usage rules

  • Body text on white uses Ink. Secondary text uses Ink soft. Captions and meta data use Ink mute.
  • Section dividers and card borders use Line. Heavier framing or eyebrow rules use Line strong.
  • Dark sections invert: Ink as background, white as primary, #cfcfcf as body text.
  • No accent color is permitted in marketing surfaces. Reserved space exists in the system if a future accent is ever introduced.

06 / Spacing & layout

A 4-pixel rhythm

Use multiples of 4. Sections breathe at 80–100px. The container caps at 1400px with a 24px gutter; never let content kiss the viewport edges.

Container

  • Max width: 1400px (--max-width).
  • Horizontal gutter: 24px (--gutter). Halves to 16px below 560px viewports.
  • Section vertical rhythm: 100px desktop, 64px mobile.
  • Body line-length: hard cap at 70ch for paragraphs, 60ch for ledes, 22ch for display titles.

08 / Patterns

How blocks combine

Patterns are reusable arrangements of components. Use them as the default — only depart from them with a documented reason.

Hero

  • Light surface (--color-surface). One sentence headline. One sentence sub. Max two buttons.
  • Eyebrow above the headline, separated by a thin top rule.
  • Min height: 72vh. Content aligns bottom-left. Hero should never feel busy.

Split

Onze aanpak

Geen copy-paste. Wel karakter.

Use a split when the message benefits from a paired visual. Visual on the right by default; alternate with .split--reverse for rhythm across a long page.

.split > .rich + .split__visual

Callout strip

Klaar voor een fiets die bij jou past?

Page header

  • Use the page header (.page-head) on every page except the home page.
  • Crumb on top, large title, optional lede. Bottom border = thin line, never a thick rule.
09 / Imagery & iconography

Documentary, not heroic

Photography is candid, natural-light, and human. Riders look like riders — not athletes mid-podium. Bikes look used, not staged.

Photography

  • Natural light. No filters that crush blacks or saturate skin tones.
  • Studio interiors, hands-on moments, side profiles of bikes — not raceday action.
  • People comfortable, neutral expressions. No forced smiles.
  • Crop tight. Negative space matters more than the subject filling the frame.

Iconography

  • Line icons only. 1.5px stroke, rounded caps and joins.
  • Monochrome — Ink on light, white on dark.
  • Match the dashed "fit-line" idiom of the logo when an icon represents measurement or analysis.
  • Don't use filled symbols, gradients, or color-coded states.
10 / Tokens

CSS custom properties

The single source of truth lives in styles.css on :root. Always reference tokens — never hard-code colors or fonts in components.

/* Color */
--color-bg: #ffffff;
--color-ink: #1f1f1f;
--color-ink-soft: #565656;
--color-ink-mute: #888888;
--color-line: #e6e6e6;
--color-line-strong: #c8c8c8;
--color-surface: #f4f4f4;
--color-surface-2: #ebebeb;

/* Type */
--font-display: 'Barlow Condensed', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif;
--font-body: 'Inter', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif;

/* Layout */
--max-width: 1400px;
--gutter: 24px;

When the system grows: add the token first, document it on this page in the matching section, and only then use it in a component. New tokens require a short rationale in the PR description.
